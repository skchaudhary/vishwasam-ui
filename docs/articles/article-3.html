Writing practical how-to articles that help readers

Published: Aug 15, 2026

How-to articles remain one of the most valuable content formats on the web: they solve specific reader problems, attract organic search traffic, and are easy to link to from social media. This guide explains a reproducible approach to craft clear, useful, and trustworthy how-to posts that perform well for readers and for services like Google AdSense.

1. Start with a clear goal

Every how-to article should answer a single, focused question. Before you write, define the reader persona and the problem they need solved. For example: "How to publish a static site to GitHub Pages" is a better-focused topic than "web publishing".

2. Use a logical step-by-step structure

Break the task into numbered steps. Readers scanning your article should be able to jump to the step they need. Each step should be short, actionable, and include example commands or screenshots where helpful. Use headings for each major step and include clear preconditions (what the reader needs before starting).

3. Provide exact commands and sample outputs

When appropriate, include copy-paste-ready commands and show example output or expected screenshots. This reduces friction for readers and makes the article more practical. Avoid proprietary or secret values — use placeholders such as your-domain.com or GITHUB_TOKEN.

4. Add context, not just steps

Explain why a step is needed and what could go wrong. This helps readers who encounter errors and increases the article's perceived expertise. Include links to official docs for deeper reading.

5. Keep it original and human-written

AdSense and search engines value original, human-created content. Avoid publishing thin, autogenerated text. Where you borrow ideas, summarize and add your own experience or examples.

6. Editorial checklist

  • Title: descriptive and includes the task.
  • At least 500 words with clear subheadings.
  • One or more examples or code snippets.
  • Privacy/contact info visible on the site.
  • Readable on mobile (short paragraphs and headings).

7. Final tips

Update articles over time when tools or commands change. Internal linking to related guides increases user engagement. If you plan to run ads, keep the content prominent and avoid excessive interstitials or ad placements that interfere with reading.
