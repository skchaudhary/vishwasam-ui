Improving site crawlability for search engines

Published: Aug 15, 2026

This article covers simple, effective ways to make your site easier for search engines — and AdSense — to crawl.

Recommendations

  • Provide clear, crawlable HTML content on each page (avoid requiring JavaScript to render essential text).
  • Include a `privacy.html` and contact page so Google can verify site ownership.
  • Ensure `robots.txt` does not block crawlers and publish a `sitemap.xml`.
  • Use descriptive titles and meta descriptions for every page.
